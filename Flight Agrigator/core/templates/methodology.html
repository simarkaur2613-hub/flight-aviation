Jevons Index (Geometric)

Standard (IMF/ONS)
I_J = ∏ (p_i^t / p_i^0) ^ (1/N)

Computes the geometric mean of price relatives. Satisfies the time reversal test (I^{0:t} × I^{t:0} = 1) and transitivity. Accounts for consumer elasticity and flight departure time substitution.

Dutot Index (Ratio of Means)

Homogeneous Only
I_D = (∑ p_i^t / N) / (∑ p_i^0 / N)

Calculates the ratio of arithmetic mean prices. Satisfies transitivity but is sensitive to ticket price levels (more expensive premium flights carry disproportionate weight).

Carli Index (Arithmetic)

Biased / Discredited
I_C = ∑ (p_i^t / p_i^0) / N

Calculates the arithmetic mean of price relatives. Fails the time reversal test; strictly greater than or equal to Jevons due to Jensen's inequality, generating systematic upward inflation drift in volatile scanner/scraped data.
