EXECUTIVE POLICY BRIEF: REAL-TIME AIRFARE PRICE INDEX (AFPI) FOR CPI AUGMENTATION

Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement ID: SIH26056 • Team: Polarchy • Date: {{ report_date }}

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Under the current Consumer Price Index (CPI Base 2012=100) compiled by MoSPI, civil aviation passenger fares are classified within the Transport and Communication sub-group (expenditure weight ~3.2%). Traditionally, price quotes are recorded manually at periodic intervals from a small sample of designated travel agents. In today's algorithmic civil aviation marketplace, carriers (led by IndiGo with ~61.5% domestic market share, Air India, Akasa Air, and SpiceJet) utilize high-frequency dynamic yield management models. As a consequence, prices diverge sharply based on booking horizons ($T-0$ to $T-30$ days), day-of-week, festive seasonality (e.g. Diwali, Chhath), and route capacity constraints.

This project by Team Polarchy establishes an automated, high-frequency web scraping pipeline harvesting daily atomic ticket quotes across major Indian airline portals and online travel aggregators (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Yatra, Cleartrip), standardized and validated through IQR outlier filtering, and aggregated using axiomatic econometric index number formulas (Jevons, Dutot, Carli, Fisher, Törnqvist).

2. Econometric Framework & Axiomatic Selection

In concordance with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and Eurostat CPI Manuals, the elementary price aggregation is executed using the Jevons Geometric Mean Index:

I_{J}^{0:t} = \prod_{i=1}^{N} \left(\frac{p_i^t}{p_i^0}\right)^{1/N} = \frac{\exp\left(\frac{1}{N}\sum \ln p_i^t\right)}{\exp\left(\frac{1}{N}\sum \ln p_i^0\right)}

Our empirical results confirm that the traditional arithmetic Carli Index exhibits a systematic upward bias (Jensen's inequality) on volatile airfare data, whereas Jevons satisfies both the time reversal test ($I^{0:t} \times I^{t:0} = 1$) and transitivity.

4. Policy Recommendations for MoSPI Advisory Committee

  1. Institutionalize Automated Web Scraping: Transition the CPI collection methodology for domestic aviation from manual field visits to automated daily microdata extraction following the Polarchy architecture.
  2. Adopt Lead-Time Stratified Weighting: Incorporate advance booking horizons ($T-0$, $T-3$, $T-7$, $T-14$, $T-30$) weighted by empirical passenger booking curves to avoid last-minute ticket oversampling.
  3. Adopt DGCA City-Pair Passenger Weighting: Apply Directorate General of Civil Aviation passenger traffic volumes as dynamic high-level weights ($W_r$) to reflect real demand patterns.
  4. Standardize on Jevons Axiomatic Metric: Formally replace arithmetic averages with the geometric Jevons formulation to eliminate Carli substitution drift.
